Anatomy & States
Every interactive element is at least 44px on its shortest touchable side. Cards are the exception that proves it — a hand card is 68×98 with a 41px stride, so the visible sliver of a covered card is still 41px wide and the whole card is reachable once it lifts.
Playing card
68×98 in hand, 56×80 on the table, 42×60 as the trump marker. Corner index is rank over suit, mirrored at 180° so the card reads from either side. The centre pip is the one element that survives at thumbnail size.
