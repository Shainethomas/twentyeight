Easing tokens
Four curves cover the product. If an interaction seems to need a fifth, it belongs to one of these.
ease-settlecubic-bezier(.2,.9,.25,1) — things arriving: deals, sheets, rows, stamps
ease-snapcubic-bezier(.22,1,.3,1) — direct response to touch: lift, press, toggle
ease-swapcubic-bezier(.5,0,.3,1) — one state replacing another: flip, score roll
linearcontinuous loops only: turn ring, progress fills
Duration scale
Anything the player caused resolves under 250ms. Anything the game narrates may take longer.
instant · 120mspress, toggle knob, tab swap
quick · 180–240mscard lift, illegal shake, chip select
normal · 340–420msflip, sheet rise, score roll, row cascade
narrative · 500–900msdeal sequence, trick collect, verdict stamp
ambient · 2–6ssweep, turn ring, crest bob, reward breathe
Reduced motion: ambient loops stop, the deal collapses to a 120ms fade-in, and particle bursts become a single 200ms gold flash on the winning pile. Nothing becomes unreadable and no state is lost.
