DESIGN SYSTEM · v1.0
Rosewood & Gold
The visual language for a premium four-player Twenty-Eight table. Warm rosewood and oxblood ground the play surface; gold is reserved for state that matters — whose turn it is, what the bid is, what you can act on.
Colour
Every surface is a warm neutral. Gold is an accent, never a background — if two things on screen are gold, one of them is wrong.

Signature elements
The parts that make a screenshot unmistakably this game and not Call Break, Spades or Euchre. Each one is reused across screens until it reads as furniture rather than decoration.
20 BID
Bid token
A milled-edge coin, not a number in a box. Sits at the centre of the scoreboard the whole hand and travels to the caller's avatar when the bidding closes.
House emblem
Engraved into the felt at 20% opacity, never as a floating logo. Repeats on the card back, the loading screen and the app icon.
Trump card
The face-down trump gets its own pulsing aura and a gold banner — it is the loudest object on the table until it flips.
Suit treatment
Solid fills on cream for play, struck into gold for the trump-caller coin. The called suit is the only suit ever rendered in metal.
Naming. Table furniture borrows the vocabulary of a South Indian card room rather than a casino: the bid is called, the trump is kept face down, a hand is a hand and not a round, and the four regulars keep their names between sessions. No chips, no dealers, no felt-green baize.
